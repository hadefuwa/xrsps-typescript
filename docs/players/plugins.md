# Plugins & Sidebar

The right-hand sidebar is a **RuneLite-style plugin system** built into the browser client. It gives you quality-of-life tools that enhance gameplay without changing the server.

## Opening the sidebar

Click any icon on the right edge of the screen to open that plugin's panel. Click the same icon again (or the arrow) to close it.

**F1** hides and shows both the sidebar and the developer tools panel entirely.

## Available plugins

### Plugin Hub
The first icon — the settings panel shown when you open the sidebar. Lists all available plugins with enable/disable toggles. **States persist in your browser's local storage** so your preferences survive page refreshes.

---

### Ground Items
Highlights item drops on the floor with coloured labels. Useful for spotting drops in the middle of combat or on busy tiles.

- Highlights items with customisable colours
- Filters out items you don't care about
- Recolours labels by item value or rarity

---

### Interact Highlight
Outlines world objects and NPCs when you hover over them or interact with them. Makes it much easier to click small or overlapping objects.

---

### Tile Markers
Shows two things:
- **Destination tile** — where your character is walking to (blue tile)
- **True tile** — your character's actual current position (different from the visual position during movement)

Useful for precise positioning in combat or puzzle content.

---

### Notes
A persistent notepad stored in your browser. Write anything — task lists, coordinates, reminders. Survives page refreshes via localStorage.

---

## Keyboard shortcuts

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| **F1** | Toggle sidebar + dev panel (login screen only) |
| **F1–F12** | Switch sidebar tabs in-game (OSRS standard) |
